Open the inspector on a page you built and you should recognize it. The same sections, the same classes, the same order — no wrapper divs you didn't write, no generated names you can't trace.

## Transparency is a feature

When the rendered DOM matches the authored markup, debugging gets shallow. Anyone — a teammate, a future you, an AI agent — can look at the output and know exactly which line produced it.

That transparency is the quiet payoff of minimalism. Less between you and the page means less to learn, less to break, and less to explain.
