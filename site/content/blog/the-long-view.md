Small sites become big sites one section at a time. The systems that survive that growth aren't the ones with the most features — they're the ones whose early decisions don't need to be unmade.

## Decisions that age well

Fluid type that scales between two values needs no breakpoint audits later. Tokens resolved through `light-dark()` never need a dark-mode retrofit. A twelve-column grid with smart stacking defaults handles layouts you haven't designed yet.

> Scalability is mostly the art of not painting yourself into corners.

Pick defaults that keep working at ten pages and at a thousand, and growth stops being a rewrite.
