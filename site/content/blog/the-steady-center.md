Every ship needs its mast — not because it's complex or clever, but because it's essential. The same is true of the code at the middle of a project: the layout system, the tokens, the handful of components everything else leans on.

## Boring on purpose

The center of a system earns trust by being predictable. A `.section` should always pad the same way. A `.col-4` should always span four columns. When the foundations stop surprising you, all of your attention goes to the parts of the work that should be surprising.

- Tokens change in one place
- Classes mean one thing
- Components do one job

> Stability isn't the absence of change. It's change that happens where you expect it.

Build the steady center once, and every page after it gets faster to make.
